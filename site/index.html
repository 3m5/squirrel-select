Browsers and devices

Three paths, by device

The browser decides how much can be styled. The closed field follows your tokens everywhere. The open list is styled only where the browser allows it and where it takes nothing away from people.

  • Fully styled Your browser

    Field and list come from SquirrelSelect, with checkmark, groups and dark mode.

    With mouse or trackpad in Chrome and Edge 135+, Safari 27+

  • Field styled Your browser

    The field matches your design. The operating system draws the list and takes over the basic colors.

    Firefox on the desktop and older browsers

  • Phone picker Your browser

    The field matches your design. Choosing opens the phone’s familiar picker, on purpose.

    Touch devices with iOS and Android
